Excelerator Password

Excelerator Password is a macOS utility for locking and unlocking supported Microsoft Excel workbook files using saved client passwords.

Privacy Policy Support

Privacy

Workbook processing occurs locally on the Mac. Workbook contents and workbook passwords are not uploaded to the developer.
