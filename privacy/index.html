Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current Excelerator Password 2.0.0 product behavior. Excelerator Password is designed for local workbook processing on your Mac. Workbook contents and workbook passwords are not uploaded to the developer.

Owner and Contact

Excelerator Password is owned and operated by Justin Matthew Strickler.

Privacy questions may be sent to jmsdesignnyc@gmail.com.

Information Processed Locally

Excelerator Password may process these items locally on your Mac:

  • User-selected Excel workbooks.
  • Workbook passwords entered or saved by the user.
  • Client profile names created by the user.
  • Application settings and preferences.
  • Local processing status and local diagnostic log messages.
  • Temporary files created during workbook processing.

Workbook Contents

Workbook contents remain local to your Mac. Excelerator Password does not upload workbook contents to a developer server, analytics service, advertising service, cloud sync service, or third-party processing service. Legacy .xls modernization is also performed locally.

Passwords and Keychain

Workbook passwords are used locally to lock or unlock workbooks. Saved client passwords are stored using macOS Keychain. During workbook processing, a password is handled transiently in local application memory and local process communication as needed to perform the requested operation. This is not a second persistent password store. Passwords are not logged and are not transmitted to the developer.

Client Profiles and Settings

Client profile names and application settings are stored locally on your Mac. They are not synchronized to a developer server.

Logs and Temporary Files

Local logs may be created for application operation and troubleshooting. Temporary processing files are created locally and the app attempts to clean them after processing. Final workbook files remain under your control at the selected file location.

No Analytics, Tracking, Advertising, or Cloud Sync

Current source inspection found no product analytics, telemetry, advertising, cloud sync, crash-reporting service, workbook upload, tracking code, cookies, or developer-operated product server call. Help, Privacy Policy, and Support links may open external web pages using your normal network connection and browser.

No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

Excelerator Password does not sell personal information. The app does not share workbook contents, workbook passwords, client profiles, usage data, or diagnostics with the developer or with third parties.

Removing Local Data

You can delete workbook files directly in Finder. The app's client profile controls request removal of saved credentials from macOS Keychain, and you can also manage saved passwords using macOS Keychain tools.

Excelerator Password also includes Clear All App Data in Settings. This removes local client profiles, removes associated saved Keychain passwords for those client profiles, resets app-owned local settings and state, and clears app-owned temporary data where possible. Clear All App Data does not delete user workbook files, processed workbook files, converted sibling .xlsx files, or unrelated Keychain items.

Security

The app uses local workbook encryption and decryption for supported Excel files. Saved passwords use macOS Keychain storage. The app does not provide cloud backup, remote account management, or remote password recovery.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated for future versions. Updates should reflect the behavior of the shipped application.
